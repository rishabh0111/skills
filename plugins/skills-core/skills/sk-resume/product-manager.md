# Product management résumés

Extra depth for [`sk-resume`](SKILL.md) when the target role is product management. Apply it on top of the checklist; the check numbers refer to it.

## What each level is judged on

- **APM and early career:** potential. Evidence of product thinking in coursework, internships and side projects ("Built and launched [project], reached 500 beta users, used retention data to iterate on the core feature").
- **Product manager and senior PM:** impact. Shipped outcomes with numbers.
- **Director and above:** scale. Portfolio, organisation and business results.

Emphasise outcomes over outputs and influence over authority at every level.

## Evidence the bullets should carry (checks 3, 7, 8)

- Revenue or growth the person moved: product managers are often accountable for it, so make it visible.
- Discovery: user interviews, research synthesis, and the decision it changed ("Ran 25 user interviews; reprioritised the roadmap toward retention, cutting churn 18%").
- Data analysis, roadmap prioritisation, competitive analysis, business metrics.
- Technical credibility: working with engineering, data and design on trade-offs.
- Frameworks the person actually used: OKRs, jobs to be done, design thinking.
- Product vocabulary: shipped, launched, led discovery, defined strategy.

## Fit by role focus (check 6)

- **Strategy roles:** vision setting, long-term outcomes.
- **Execution roles:** delivery, operational excellence.
- **Cross-functional roles:** stakeholder alignment, influence.

## Titles (check 11)

Standard titles: Associate Product Manager, Product Manager, Product Manager II, Senior Product Manager, Principal Product Manager, Group or Director of Product.

**Product owner and product manager.** Product owner is an accountability in Scrum; product manager is a job title. When the official title was product owner but the person worked as a full product manager (direct access to customers, stakeholders, engineers and designers, with no proxies), recommend "Product Manager" on the résumé and explaining the context in interviews.

## Certifications (check 10)

Product-specific certifications and courses go after education. Tools such as SQL, analytics and design tools go in the optional technical skills section, last.
